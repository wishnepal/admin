5 PROMPTS TO CREATE PRODUCT PHOTOS

Attach a reference photo, then paste any prompt.

5 prompts
PROMPT 1

Create a premium hero product shot of the product shown in the reference photo. Place it on a clean two-tone background with cinematic directional lighting, soft realistic shadows, and one bold headline space. Keep the packaging accurate to the reference. Photorealistic, high detail, appetizing, no plastic AI look.

PROMPT 2

Create a dynamic action shot of the product shown in the reference photo, with its key ingredients and particles bursting and floating around it, frozen mid-air. Dramatic studio lighting, rich texture, true-to-life colors, photorealistic, no over-smoothing.

PROMPT 3

Create a lifestyle shot of a real, natural-looking person enjoying the product shown in the reference photo. Candid mid-action pose, genuine expression, realistic un-retouched skin with visible pores, cinematic lighting, premium advertising look. Keep the packaging accurate to the reference.

PROMPT 4

Create an extreme macro close-up of the product shown in the reference photo, emphasizing its texture and detail, with subtle particles or droplets around it. Shallow depth of field, hyper-real detail, mouth-watering, photorealistic, no AI plastic look.

PROMPT 5

Create a 9-tile brand identity grid for the product shown in the reference photo. Use a consistent two-tone palette, different real models across tiles, varied shot distances (macro, mid, wide), bold headline typography, and a small logo and handle on each tile. Keep the packaging accurate to the reference. Premium, cohesive, photorealistic.

STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW · EDIT VIDEOS WITH GOOGLE OMNI

Video Clone Prompt

Use Claude + Gemini Omni to recreate any video style with your own script. No editing skills needed.

01
THE WORKFLOW · STEP 1

Find your reference video

Instagram, Pinterest, or YouTube

Find 1-2 motion design videos whose style you love. Screen-record or save them. Also have your script ready — the exact words you want spoken in your video.
WHERE TO LOOK
"kinetic typography reel" "motion design short" "AI video edit style"
Search these on Instagram Reels or YouTube. Save anything that has the energy you want.
02
THE WORKFLOW · STEP 2

Open Claude and paste this prompt

Upload reference + your script

Upload your reference video (or screenshots from it) and paste your script. Then paste the prompt below. Claude will analyze the style and write ready-made scene prompts for you.
Paste this entire prompt into Claude 
I'm attaching a reference video and my script below.
I want to recreate this video's STYLE for my own
script using an AI video generator (Gemini Omni / Veo).
Step 1: Analyze the reference video deeply. Describe:
  - Animation style (2D, 3D, kinetic typography, flat design)
  - How things move (speed, easing, bounce, smooth or snappy)
  - Transitions (cuts, wipes, morphs, zooms)
  - Color palette (exact colors and background)
  - Typography style (bold, thin, animated, where text appears)
  - Camera behavior (static, zoom, pan, shake)
  - Overall mood and pacing (fast, calm, dramatic)
  - Sound feel (whooshes, clicks, bass hits, music style)
Step 2: Break my script into scenes of 8-10 seconds
each (because the AI generates max 10 seconds per clip).
Step 3: For EACH scene, write one detailed prompt that includes:
  - What appears on screen and how it animates
  - The exact text shown on screen (max 5-6 words)
  - Colors, background, and typography matching the reference
  - Camera movement
  - Transition into the next scene
  - Sound effects and music mood
Here is my script:
  [PASTE YOUR SCRIPT HERE]
03
THE WORKFLOW · STEP 3

Get your scene prompts from Claude

One prompt per scene, copy-ready

Claude breaks your script into individual scenes and writes a detailed prompt for each one. Each scene is 8-10 seconds — the max Gemini Omni generates per clip.

04
THE WORKFLOW · STEP 4

Generate in Gemini Omni

One scene at a time

Open the Gemini app. Select the video model — Gemini Omni Flash is the newest. Paste one scene prompt, attach your reference video, and hit generate. Repeat for each scene.
PRO TIP
Always attach your reference video or raw footage clip to each generation. Without it, Omni invents the style from scratch instead of matching yours.
05
THE WORKFLOW · STEP 5

Iterate with chat commands

Fix anything in plain language

If a scene looks wrong, just tell Gemini in the chat. It will redo that scene. Keep the best version of each clip.
EXAMPLE FIX COMMANDS
"Make the text bigger" "Slow down the animation" "Change the background color to red" "Add more glitch effect on the transition" "Make the text slam in harder"
06
THE WORKFLOW · STEP 6

Assemble in CapCut

Add music, voiceover, and export

Drop all your scenes into CapCut (or any editor), arrange them in order, add music or voiceover, and export. Done.
Important: If any on-screen text looks messy or wrong, redo that text directly in CapCut or Figma. AI still struggles with perfectly clean text — always double-check before posting.
